# Kalinga — Hackathon Pitch

## The Hook (10 seconds)
Every family runs on a patchwork of calendars, apps, and sticky notes — one for school, one for a parent's meds, one for pickups. Nothing shows the whole family's day in one place.

## The Problem
Coordinating a family means juggling multiple people's schedules across multiple tools. Tasks get assigned verbally or over text, and there's no easy way to confirm they actually got done — did the kid really take their meds? Did dad remember the dentist appointment?

## The Solution
**Kalinga** is one shared, color-coded timeline for the whole family. Everyone can add their own schedule, parents can assign tasks to specific family members, a daily overview shows exactly who's done what today — even offline — and everyone can share how they're feeling, not just what they're doing.

## What We Built Today (1-hour demo)
1. **Merged Timeline** — every family member's day in one color-coded, time-sorted list
2. **Task Assignment, visible on the timeline** — tasks clearly tagged "Assigned by Mom," proving the core accountability concept
3. **Role Switcher** — instantly view the day from any family member's perspective
4. **Mark as Done** — check off a task live
5. **Daily Overview** — an at-a-glance summary of who's done what today, computed entirely from local data — no internet required
6. **Mood Indicator** — an emoji next to each person's name showing how they're feeling today (happy, sad, celebrating, neutral)

## Demo Flow
1. **Show the chaos** — describe the "before": separate apps/notes for each family member
2. **Reveal the merged timeline** — one screen, color-coded, sorted by time
3. **Point to an assigned task** — "Take meds — assigned by Mom" tag, live on screen
4. **Switch roles** — flip to the kid's view, check off the task
5. **Show the daily overview** — "Mom: 2/3 done · Jayden: 1/2 done" updates instantly
6. **Point out the mood emojis** — "Kalinga isn't just tracking tasks, it's showing how everyone's actually doing today"
7. **Close with the vision** — self-scheduling, reminders, and photo proof as the next steps beyond today's build

## Why This Wins
- **Relatable pain point** — every family, not a niche audience
- **Clear before/after visual** — strong demo moment (scattered → merged)
- **Task assignment is the differentiator** — this isn't just a calendar, it's an accountability loop between family members
- **Honest, focused scope** — a tight, working 1-hour proof-of-concept backed by a clear, larger product vision

## Closing Line
> "Kalinga isn't just a shared calendar — it's how the whole family assigns, tracks, and follows through on each other's day, and knows how everyone's really doing along the way."
